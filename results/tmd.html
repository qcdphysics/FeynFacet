TMD beam functions and soft function

The NLO matching coefficients $I_{qq}, I_{qg}, I_{gq}, I_{gg}$ of the TMD beam functions, quark and gluon soft functions, and soft-subtracted TMDPDF matching coefficients, with the exponential rapidity regulator.

Wilson lines and operator insertions are fields of a FeynArts model, so the operator matrix elements are generated like cross sections. The Fourier transforms become Euler integrals reduced with Kira and evaluated with SubTropica.

Checks

Exact agreement with the ancillary files of Luo, Yang, Zhu and Zhu, JHEP 06 (2021) 115; Ward identities of the generated currents; the dependence on the rapidity scale cancels between the beam and soft functions; the three algebra routes agree.
