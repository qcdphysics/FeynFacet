# Partonic Born cross sections: every massless 2 → 2 QCD channel in D dimensions

The lowest-order hard cross sections of all massless two-parton scattering
channels a b → c d with a tagged (observed) final-state parton c, in
D = 4 − 2ε dimensions, for the spin average (UU) and for the
double-longitudinal helicity difference of the incoming partons (LL).  There
are 29 channels: quark–quark, quark–antiquark, quark–gluon and gluon–gluon
scattering, with their charge conjugates and the permutations of the incoming
legs (t is defined with the first incoming parton, so a b → c and b a → c are
separate entries).  The result, `Results/Born.wl`, is the table of |M|²/g_s⁴,
averaged over the spins and colours of a and b and summed over those of c and
d, as functions of s, t (u = −s−t), D, C_F and C_A.  It provides the Born
terms and the lower-order ingredients of the collinear counterterms of
`Processes/Hadronic`, and the resolved-photon Born terms of the lepton
processes.  (The transverse spin transfers needed for the Collins effect are
computed in `Processes/Collins`.)

The table is computed three times from the same FeynArts amplitudes, with
three independent implementations of the spin, Lorentz and colour algebra:
FeynCalc (the main route, `1-Born.wl`), FORM and Tracer.  The FORM and Tracer
routes must reproduce the FeynCalc table exactly.

## Files

| File | What it does | Kernel time |
|---|---|---|
| `1-Born.wl` | FeynArts diagrams of the 29 channels; squares with FeynCalc (`Results/Born.wl`); comparison with the textbook table; the amplitudes as plain records for the other routes (`Results/Amplitudes.wl`) | 8 min |
| `2-Algebra-FORM.wl` | The same table with FORM and its colour library `color.h` (`Results/FORM/Born.wl`); the program is in `form/` | 1 min |
| `2-Algebra-Tracer.wl` | The same table with Tracer and an explicit SU(N) colour reduction (`Results/Tracer/Born.wl`) | 3.5 min |
| `form/` | `common.h` (complex conjugation, sewing of the fermion lines into traces, spin densities with the HVBM γ₅, gluon polarization sums, kinematics) and `Born.frm` (the loop over the channels, the colour sums with `color.h`, the γ₅ normalization check) |
| `*.nb` | Notebook versions of the `.wl` files, generated by `Checks/make_notebooks.wls` |
| `Results/` | Created when the notebooks run (not part of the repository) |

## The three routes

* **FeynCalc** (`1-Born.wl`): the amplitudes from `FCFAConvert`; the quark
  spinors closed by `FermionSpinSum`, the traces by `DiracSimplify`, the
  colour sums by `SUNSimplify`.  The same amplitudes are then written as plain
  records (one coefficient and the open fermion chains per term), so that the
  other two routes square exactly the same expressions.
* **FORM** (`2-Algebra-FORM.wl`, `form/Born.frm`): the records are written to
  `amplitudes.inc` in the conventions of FORM's colour library.  For each
  channel the program multiplies the amplitude by its complex conjugate, sums
  over all colours with `color.h` (before any Dirac algebra) and sets the
  group invariants to their SU(N_c) values, closes the fermion lines into one
  or two traces (the quark lines of two interfering diagrams may connect
  differently, e.g. the t- and u-channel graphs of q q → q q), inserts the
  spin densities, takes the D-dimensional traces and contracts all indices.
  γ₅ is written explicitly as −(i/24) ε_{μνρσ} γ^μγ^νγ^ργ^σ with
  four-dimensional indices.  The program runs once for UU and once for LL
  (TFORM, three threads).
* **Tracer** (`2-Algebra-Tracer.wl`): the traces with Tracer's `G`/`Spur`
  and `AntiCommute[off]` (HVBM γ₅).  The fermion lines are closed by following
  the spinor momenta from chain to chain, the same rule as in FORM.  The colour
  sums are done by a short reduction written out in the notebook, from three
  identities: f^{abc} = −2i(Tr[TᵃTᵇTᶜ] − Tr[TᵇTᵃTᶜ]), the Fierz identity
  (Tᵃ)_{ij}(Tᵃ)_{kl} = ½(δ_{il}δ_{kj} − δ_{ij}δ_{kl}/N_c), and δ_{ii} = N_c,
  δ^{aa} = N_c²−1.  Each distinct pair of colour structures is evaluated once.

## How to run

Open the files in Mathematica, `1-Born.wl` first, then either of the
`2-Algebra-*.wl` files, each in a fresh kernel (Tracer cannot share a kernel
with FeynCalc), and evaluate the cells from top to bottom (`Shift+Enter`).
The two `2-Algebra-*.wl` files read `Results/Amplitudes.wl` and
`Results/Born.wl` and write their own table under `Results/FORM/` or
`Results/Tracer/`; they can be rerun on their own once `1-Born.wl` has run.

Headless, from the repository root:

```sh
python Checks/run_process.py Processes/PartonicBorn --route FeynCalc   # 1-Born only
python Checks/run_process.py Processes/PartonicBorn --route FORM       # 1-Born, then the FORM route
python Checks/run_process.py Processes/PartonicBorn --route Tracer     # 1-Born, then the Tracer route
```

(`--steps 2` runs only the second step), or `RunSteps["PartonicBorn", "FORM"]`
from a notebook with FeynFacet loaded.  A completed run means that every check
below passed.

## What is checked, and where

All checks are visible `Require` cells; a failed check stops the run.

| Step | Check |
|---|---|
| 1 | the four-dimensional UU results equal the textbook table (Ellis, Stirling and Webber, *QCD and Collider Physics*, Table 7.1); the LL results of the quark channels follow helicity conservation, those of q g → q and g g → q their known forms; the crossing relations between incoming-leg permutations (t ↔ u) hold for UU and LL |
| 2 (FORM) | the explicit HVBM γ₅ reproduces Tr[γᵃγᵇγᶜγᵈγ₅] = −4i ε^{abcd}; no colour object, polarization vector, open fermion chain or unknown propagator is left (otherwise FORM stops); every channel, UU and LL, equals the FeynCalc table identically in s, t, D and N_c |
| 2 (Tracer) | Tracer's γ₅ gives Tr[γᵃγᵇγᶜγᵈγ₅] = +4i ε^{abcd}; the colour reduction reproduces Tr(TᵃTᵃ) = C_F N_c, f^{abc}f^{abc} = N_c(N_c²−1), Tr(TᵃTᵇTᵇTᵃ) = C_F² N_c, Tr(TᵃTᵇTᵃTᵇ) = −C_F/2 and the sign of f^{abc} Tr(TᶜTᵇTᵃ); every fermion line closes; every channel, UU and LL, equals the FeynCalc table identically in s, t, D and N_c |

The comparison with the FeynCalc table is exact: with C_F = (N_c²−1)/(2N_c)
and C_A = N_c both tables are rational functions of s, t, D and N_c, and their
difference must vanish channel by channel.  The saved FORM and Tracer tables,
rewritten with C_F and C_A, are checked against it once more.

## Conventions in brief

* Kinematics: a(p_a) + b(p_b) → c(p) + d(p_d), s = (p_a+p_b)², t = (p_a−p)²,
  u = (p_b−p)², s + t + u = 0; all four momenta massless and
  four-dimensional.  The hard cross section s E dσ/d³p of the tagged parton is
  |M|²/(16π² s v) δ(1−w) with v = 1 + t/s.
* Flavours: q a quark, qp a quark of another flavour, qb and qpb their
  antiquarks, g the gluon.  For q q → q q no identical-particle factor ½ is
  applied: it is cancelled by the two ways of tagging (the convention of the
  reference codes).
* Colour: SU(N_c) with Tr(TᵃTᵇ) = δ^{ab}/2, C_F = (N_c²−1)/(2N_c),
  C_A = N_c.  Averages 1/(2N_c) per incoming quark, 1/((D−2)(N_c²−1)) (UU) or
  1/(N_c²−1) (LL) per incoming gluon.
* Spin (HVBM scheme): for LL a polarized incoming quark gets the density
  p̸γ₅/2 (−p̸γ₅/2 for an antiquark) and a polarized incoming gluon l the
  circular density i ε^{μνlr}/(2 l·r), r the other incoming momentum, with
  Tr[γᵃγᵇγᶜγᵈγ₅] = −4i ε^{abcd} (FeynCalc, `$LeviCivitaSign = −1`); LL is
  (σ(++) − σ(+−))/2.  Every other gluon is summed with
  −g^{μν} + (l^μ r^ν + r^μ l^ν)/(l·r), r = p_b for p_a and r = p_a for
  p_b, p, p_d.  Tracer's γ₅ has the opposite sign, so its circular density
  carries the opposite sign too; every LL term contains exactly two such
  objects, and the table is unchanged.  FORM contracts two ε tensors with the
  Euclidean sign; the LL squares, with exactly two ε tensors per term, are
  multiplied by −1.
