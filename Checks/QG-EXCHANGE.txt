INTERFERENCE CENSUS AND EXCHANGE IN q g -> q g g

Checks/QGExchange.wls reads only the FeynCalc-route class-13 subprocess
cache files real-q_g_q_g_g-{UU,LL}-i-j.wl. These are products of a
16-amplitude basis. For each spin projection the complete upper triangle
contains 16 diagonal and 120 off-diagonal products. The reconstructed real
square is the sum of Re(Value) on the diagonal and 2 Re(Value) off it.
A common initial-state average or identical-particle weight has no effect
on the exchange test; the checker does not determine those overall factors.
No production squaring, real-emission summation or integration helper is
loaded, and other class-13 quark-pair subprocesses are not included.

There are 12 literal exact-zero generic cached products per spin. The other
124 are proved nonzero expressions by exhibiting a finite nonzero rational
value at at least one of the three points below. This census does not
identify whether any zero originates in colour orthogonality or spin algebra.

For k1+k2=pa+pb-p, exchange of the two unobserved gluons acts as

 A -> -(s+t)-A, B -> -(s+u)-B, C -> -t-u-C=s-s4-C,
 s4=s+t+u.

The map preserves A+B+C=-s4 and is an involution; these are symbolic map
identities. The real-square equality itself is sampled at D=4, spe3=0,
gs=1, at these exact-rational (s,t,u,A,B,C,Nc) points:

 (17,-7,-6,-2,-5,3,3), (23,-8,-9,-4,-6,4,5),
 (31,-12,-11,-5,-9,6,7).

All individual interference values must be finite rational numbers on both
sides, and each point must satisfy the phase-space constraint. At the
separate point (29,-11,-10,-3,-9,4,3), the incorrect map C->s-C must yield
unequal finite sums; singular values cannot pass this negative control.
All 272 input file hashes are checked before and after the comparisons.

After producing the class-13 FeynCalc algebra caches, run from the repository
root with a separate output directory:

 FEYNFACET_SOURCE="$PWD" FEYNFACET_BENCHMARK_OUTPUT="../qg-exchange-check" \
   math -noprompt -script Checks/QGExchange.wls

The outputs are exchange.wl, summary.json and incremental progress.json.
Keep generated outputs outside the source release.
