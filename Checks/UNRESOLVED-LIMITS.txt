SOFT, COLLINEAR AND EXCHANGE CHECKS OF SELECTED HADRONIC ALGEBRA

Scope
The checker reads the FeynCalc-route Algebra.wl files for ordinary hadronic
class1, transverse-transfer class1 and ordinary hadronic class15. It checks
qq' -> qq'g soft and incoming-quark collinear limits for UU, LL and transfer
XX,NN, and exchange of the two unobserved gluons in gg -> ggg for UU,LL.
The splitting kernels and spin algebra are four-dimensional. The three-gluon
sector is selected by setting Nf=0 in class15; its weight 1/2 is retained.
The soft and collinear identities retain generic kinematic ratios. Exchange
is checked at three saved exact-rational points, rather than symbolically.
No amplitude generation, integration or finite-assembly helper is loaded.

Colour correlations from Fierz identities
Let D=delta31 delta42 and X=delta32 delta41 for qq' -> qq'. The Born colour
state is M=(X-D/Nc)/2, with scalar products <D,D>=<X,X>=Nc^2,<D,X>=Nc.
The colour norm is (Nc^2-1)/4. The fundamental identity

 T^a_ij T^a_kl = (delta_il delta_kj-delta_ij delta_kl/Nc)/2

gives these matrices in the ordered basis {D,X}:

 T1.T2 = (1/2) {{-1/Nc,1},{1,-1/Nc}},
 T1.T3 = {{-CF,-1/2},{0,1/(2Nc)}}.

Incoming charges have the incoming-line minus sign. Taking expectation values
in M yields r12=-1/Nc and r13=1/(2Nc). Colour conservation gives
r14=-CF-r12-r13=-(Nc^2-2)/(2Nc); the remaining pairs follow by line exchange.
The checker evaluates these colour-flow matrix elements exactly in Nc.

Soft limit
Write k2=lambda*l, qi=2pi.l, with rho=qa+qb-qc. On-shell conservation gives

 u=-s-t+lambda*rho,
 A=-s-t+lambda*qa,
 B=t-lambda*rho+lambda*qb,
 C=s-lambda*rho-lambda*qc.

The sum A+B+C remains -(s+t+u). Squaring the eikonal current gives
-2gs^2 Sum_{i<j}rij*pi.pj/(pi.k2 pj.k2). Thus lambda^2*Real approaches
Born times

 -4gs^2 [r12*s/(qa*qb)+r13*(-t)/(qa*qc)
         +r14*(s+t)/(qa*rho)+r14*(s+t)/(qb*qc)
         +r13*(-t)/(qb*rho)+r12*s/(qc*rho)].

The same soft current multiplies every listed Born spin projection.

Incoming-quark collinear limit
For k2 -> (1-w)pa, define delta=-2pa.k2. The exact invariant map is

 A=-(s+t)-delta, B=w*t, C=s*w+delta,
 u=-(s+t)w, s4=(s+t)(1-w).

The hard Born process has incoming momentum w pa, hence invariants
(s',t',u')=(w*s,w*t,u). The initial-state splitting normalization is

 delta*Real -> -2gs^2/w * P(w) * Born(s',t',u').

The sign follows from delta=-2pa.k2; 1/w is the initial-state spin-density
and flux normalization. Helicity-conserving splitting amplitudes give
P_qq=CF(1+w^2)/(1-w) for UU and LL. Their transverse-helicity interference
gives delta P_qq=2CF*w/(1-w) for XX and NN. These are ordinary kernels in
0<w<1, before plus-distribution completion and virtual endpoint terms.

Generic ratios and leading powers
Hard-scale homogeneity sets s=1,t=-v. The arbitrary normalization of l can be
absorbed in lambda, so qa=1 while qb/qa and qc/qa remain generic. For arbitrary
s and qa, restore the soft coefficient's factor s/qa^2. This is not a sampled
soft direction. Likewise delta*Real is dimensionless, so s=1 retains the full
collinear dependence on v,w. The combined rational numerator and denominator
are required to be polynomials in the limiting parameter. Their lowest powers determine the
actual singularity order; a stronger pole fails rather than being discarded.
Five finite/stronger-pole/vanishing/cancellation/zero controls test the helper.

Identical-gluon exchange
With k1+k2=pa+pb-p, the exact exchange is

 A -> -(s+t)-A, B -> -(s+u)-B, C -> -t-u-C=s-s4-C.

It preserves A+B+C=-s4 and squares to the identity; these two map identities
are proved symbolically. The UU and LL real squares are compared at three
exact-rational points, listed here as (s,t,u,A,B,C,Nc), with gs=1:

 (17,-7,-6,-2,-5,3,3), (23,-8,-9,-4,-6,4,5),
 (31,-12,-11,-5,-9,6,7).

Each point and its exchanged point must satisfy the phase-space constraint
and give finite rational values. The source's identical unobserved-gluon
weight 1/2 is checked as bookkeeping; exchange symmetry alone cannot determine
that normalization. The incorrect replacement C->s-C must produce unequal
finite rational values at (29,-11,-10,-3,-9,4,3). Infinities do not count as
detection of the incorrect map.

Execution
The process steps must first have produced the three required Algebra.wl
files. From the repository root, choose an external output directory:

 FEYNFACET_SOURCE="$PWD" FEYNFACET_BENCHMARK_OUTPUT="../unresolved-check" \
   math -noprompt -script Checks/UnresolvedLimits.wls

Outputs are limits.wl, summary.json and an incremental progress.json.
Generated artifacts should remain outside the source release. Input hashes
are checked before and after execution. Finite perturbations of the soft and
collinear kernels and the incorrect exchange map exercise sensitivity.
